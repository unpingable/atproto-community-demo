The honest bits

When the source changes, the feed says so.

A community decision and an author’s source state are different facts. The renderer keeps both visible.

Changed at source

The author updated the source post.

The original community admission remains visible, but the renderer does not silently substitute the new version.

Source unavailable

The source cannot be observed right now.

Juniper Commons remembers that it admitted a reference; it does not invent replacement content.

Removed

Juniper Commons removed the item from its feed.

That community decision does not delete or rewrite the author’s original record.

The product idea

Communities can curate without taking custody.

The useful split is deliberately simple:

  • Authors own and update their source posts.
  • Communities publish inclusion and removal decisions.
  • Renderers present those decisions without becoming the authority.
