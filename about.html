About this experiment

The community owns the choice. The app is only the window.

This is a fictional, synthetic demonstration of a portable community projection. It contains no live ATProto account, Space, credential, member, or moderation action.

What it demonstrates

  • An author-owned post can be referenced without transferring ownership.
  • A community can publish its separate choice to include or remove that reference.
  • Ordinary and technical renderers can show the same lifecycle semantics.
What it does not establish: identity authenticity, reader permission, membership, moderator authority, source truth, currentness, human usability, or production readiness.
